About

I'm a senior AI engineer and data scientist with a background in statistical physics and hands-on experience in data engineering. My passion lies in exploring the fusion of software, data and AI to drive innovations that improve human life.

I've held leadership roles in data and ML/AI, including Head of AI, Chief Data Officer and Lead Data Scientist, and I have a track record of translating complex data science into user-friendly software products. I've deployed ML-driven software in heavy industry, oil and gas, advertising, marketing and behavioural research.

I hold a PhD in statistical physics from CBPF, where I investigated fluctuation relations, thermostatistics and turbulent time series, with applications in biology and finance. That work gave me depth in information dynamics and a taste for problems where theory has to meet real data.

I am a believer in the power of creativity to reshape technology, culture, politics and problem solving.
